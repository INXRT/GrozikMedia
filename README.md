### 📖 About Grozik Media
**Grozik Media** operates at the intersection of music culture, creator dynamics, and digital marketing. As a modern marketing agency and record label, Grozik Media is engineered to propel artists, creators, and entertainment brands into mainstream attention and sustained viral growth.

#### What We Do:
- **TikTok & Short-Form Campaigns**: Precision influencer marketing campaigns and sound seeding strategies engineered to generate high-retention TikTok, Reels, and Shorts trends.
- **Record Label & Artist Rollout Services**: Strategic campaign roadmaps from pre-save teaser phases to post-release streaming acceleration.
- **Creator Outreach Network**: Direct access to vetted content creators and tastemakers across high-engagement niches.
- **Performance & Growth Analytics**: Comprehensive campaign tracking measuring reach, streams generated, creator engagement, and streaming ROI.

### 🛠️ Technology Stack & Engineering Architecture
The web experience is crafted with an emphasis on fluid kinetic motion, dark luxury aesthetics, and performance:

- **Frontend Core**: [React 18](https://react.dev/) powered by [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Smooth Inertial Scrolling**: [Lenis Scroll Engine](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) for seamless, momentum-based scrolling
- **Animation Orchestration**: [GSAP (GreenSock)](https://greensock.com/) timeline sequences paired with [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/) micro-interactions
- **Styling Architecture**: Custom responsive dark aesthetic with metallic charcoal tones and street-culture typography
- **Edge Delivery**: Cloudflare Global Edge Network
